[Download for Mac](https://github.com/JohnDeved/orbit-disk/releases/download/v0.11.3/Orbit-Disk.dmg). Open the disk image and drag Orbit Disk.app to Applications, or use **Orbit Disk → Check for Updates…**.

Orbit Disk 0.11.3 lets you drag anything from the map or the Contents list while the map is updating.

## Fixes

- A drag no longer stops with "These items cannot be collected. Refresh the map if they have changed." The map can be behind while it updates. A drag now always starts, and the items are checked on disk when you drop them.
- Items the map has not finished identifying are collected as they are now, instead of being skipped as moved or deleted.
- When something can't be collected (the mapped folder itself, protected system or Library folders, hidden folders, or items that are gone), the drop says why for each item.

## What changes when you update

Saved maps, Places and Full Disk Access are kept. The map format is unchanged.

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/JohnDeved/orbit-disk/releases/download/v0.11.2/Orbit-Disk.dmg). Open the disk image and drag Orbit Disk.app to Applications, or use **Orbit Disk → Check for Updates…**.

Orbit Disk 0.11.2 makes exploring the map and collecting from it feel direct: hovering the map shows a tooltip at the pointer, and dragging picks up the row or segment itself. Background updates no longer get in the way of collecting.

## Map tooltip

- Hovering a segment shows a tooltip just above the pointer, in the look of a Contents row: colour, name, file count or type, size and share. Segments in outer rings also say which folder they are in.
- The map center stays on the shown folder (or your selection) instead of repeating what the tooltip says, and the hovered path no longer appears above the Contents list.

## Dragging

- Dragging picks up what you pressed. A row lifts out of the list, and a segment lifts out of the map as its own arc, both held at the point you grabbed. Large segments shrink to a size that is easy to carry, with a label showing what they are. The place they left dims.
- Over the collection tray, the dragged item turns coral and shows a plus. The tray's outline and "Release to collect" state now fade in and out.
- Dropping on the tray tosses the item into the tray icon, which pops as it arrives. Letting go anywhere else springs the item back into its place.
- While you drag, the map and the Contents rows no longer react to hover.
- With **Reduce Motion** turned on, these states change without animation.

## Collecting while the map updates

- Background updates never stop a drag. Updates on a whole-disk map can arrive every few seconds; a drag now continues even when one arrives while you press.
- A file that changed since the map read it (for example, a document an app just saved) is collected as it is now, and its folder updates in the background. Previously you had to refresh the map first. Only items that no longer exist are skipped. Removal still checks every item again just before it is moved to the Trash or deleted.

## Fixes

- "The saved map changed; refresh and try again" no longer appears after you pause updates, or switch to another location, while a background update is finishing.

## What changes when you update

Saved maps, Places and Full Disk Access are kept. The map format is unchanged.
